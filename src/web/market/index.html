STATION DETAILS

Supply is how many tons the station you buy from has for sale. A higher number skips stations that can't fill your hold.

Demand is how many tons the station you sell to still wants. When demand is low the price drops as you sell.

PRESETS

A preset is a named copy of your search settings (trip, distances, price age, station details, Powerplay). Switch between them here or from the overlay menu, without leaving the game.

OVERLAYS AND KEYS

Five overlays, each with its own key. In the game, the key shows that overlay and gives you the mouse on it; press it again to hide it, and the game has the mouse back once none is showing. On the overlay itself: hold its top strip to move it, and use "smaller" and "bigger" for its size. The Flight inputs overlay is the exception: it never takes the mouse, so you can fly with it up. Move and size it here or in Studio Flight.

Hauling overlay: its key (click, then press the keys)
Find by name overlay: its key (click, then press the keys)
Route overlay: its key (click, then press the keys)
Loadouts overlay: its key (click, then press the keys)
Flight inputs overlay: its key (click, then press the keys)

The "controller" boxes are for a stick, HOTAS or gamepad. Click one, then press a button on the controller, or hold one button and press another: a pair like that leaves every single button to the game. Click the box again to clear it. Studio only listens, so the game still sees these buttons too.

Keys and controller buttons work while Elite is the active window, so they never block other programs.

Elite must be in Borderless (Options › Graphics › Display › Window mode) for an overlay to show over the game.

Prices come from Ardent Insight by Iain Collins, built on what players share through EDDN. It is community data and can be out of date or missing in quiet areas: the Updated column says how old each price is. Powerplay information comes from Spansh and is only asked for when you set a Powerplay filter.

Studio sends those services only what a search needs: the name of the system you are in, your distances and filters, and a commodity you look up. Where you are, your ship and your cargo come from Elite's own journal files, which Studio only reads.
