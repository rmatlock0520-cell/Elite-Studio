Modified Guardian weapons · Mbooni only

Salvation's pre-engineered versions of three Guardian weapons: the gauss cannon, the plasma charger and the shard cannon, fixed and in small and medium only. The one broker that unlocks them is the Guardian technology broker at Prospect's Deep in Mbooni, and Mbooni needs a permit.

Is it worth the trip?

  • One unlock, one weapon. A weapon above, once unlocked, can be bought again and again. A modified one has to be unlocked again for every one you fit.
  • Small recipes. One Guardian Weapon Blueprint Segment and a handful of materials each (the list below), so once you have the permit they cost little.
  • Not better at everything. Players rate the modified plasma charger highly and call the modified gauss cannon a sidegrade at best. Nobody has published their numbers, so fit one and try it before you unlock a set.
  • Worth it when you already fight Thargoids with Guardian weapons and want more from the same hardpoints. For a first Guardian weapon, the ones above are unlocked at any Guardian broker and need no permit.

Getting the Mbooni permit

  1. Go to . The permit mission only appears within about 20 light years of Mbooni, and LHS 1163 is where Azimuth Biotech, the faction that gives it, keeps its megaship, the Glorious Prospect.
  2. Become Allied with Azimuth Biotech. Run their missions, or trade at their market, on the Glorious Prospect until your standing with them is Allied.
  3. Take "Permit Acquisition Opportunity". It is not on the board by itself: once you are Allied it comes as a follow-up mission (the chain icon) after another Azimuth Biotech mission, and not every time. Keep doing their missions until it shows.
  4. Fly to with the permit and land at on Mbooni 3 a, 1,490 Ls from the star (large pads). Its Guardian technology broker has the modified weapons.

The Glorious Prospect has moved before (it was in T Tauri). If it is not in LHS 1163, look for Azimuth Biotech in another system within 20 light years of Mbooni. Mbooni has also spent weeks in lockdown, when stations can close some services: look at its state before a long trip.

Studio cannot tell whether you have unlocked these: the game's names for them in the journal are not known yet, so none is ever ticked off. The recipes are Inara's, from one source and not compared like the ones above: check them at the broker before you fly out.
