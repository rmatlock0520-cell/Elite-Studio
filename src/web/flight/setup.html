TrainerStick setup

Stick check guided, about 2½ minutes

With Flight Assist off, anything the stick gives at rest keeps turning the ship faster and faster, and an old stick seldom comes back to exactly the middle. This check finds where yours rests, by itself and with your hand resting on it as in flight, and how far it reaches, then sets your Flight Assist off dead zones to match (and, for a worn stick that never reaches its end, how far it travels), with a button to put back the ones before. The trainer's exercises are then for tuning the curves. Pick the device, press Start, and follow the steps on screen: each moves on by itself.
